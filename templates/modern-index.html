What is BlackFile?

BlackFile is a secure document-sharing platform designed for privacy-conscious users who need to share sensitive files temporarily. Unlike traditional cloud storage, we don't keep your files. They're encrypted, shared once, and permanently deleted.

Built for professionals, students, and anyone who values privacy, BlackFile ensures your documents remain confidential through military-grade encryption and strict access controls.

How It Works

Simple, secure file sharing in three steps

  1. Upload & Encrypt - Select your file and recipient's email. File is encrypted with AES-256 before upload.
  2. Recipient Gets Email - They receive a secure email with download link, OTP code, and Secret Key.
  3. Download & Delete - After successful download, the file is permanently deleted from our servers.
